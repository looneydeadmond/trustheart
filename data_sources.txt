TrustHeart — Data Access Links

The two public ECG datasets used in the study, with their canonical access URLs.

1. MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database
   URL:      https://physionet.org/content/mitdb/1.0.0/
   Version:  1.0.0
   Details:  48 two-channel records, 360 Hz, AAMI five groups (N, S, V, F, Q),
             about 110,000 annotated beats; inter-patient De Chazal split.
   License:  Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0 (see the PhysioNet page).

2. CPSC2018 (China Physiological Signal Challenge 2018)
   URL:      http://2018.icbeb.org/Challenge.html
   Version:  2018 release
   Details:  6,877 twelve-lead recordings, 500 Hz, 6-60 s, nine classes
             (N, AF, I-AVB, LBBB, RBBB, PAC, PVC, STD, STE); five-fold
             patient-level cross-validation.
   License:  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (challenge data-use terms on the page above).

Both datasets are public and de-identified. No model weights were released.
